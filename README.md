## Stay in your flow

An idea, a question, a file to explore. Click Piko, choose your agent, and get to work. Your conversation stays close while your desktop stays yours.

- **One home for your agents.** Switch between Codex, Claude Code, and Hermes from a single menu bar icon.
- **Context that stays with you.** Keep separate conversations, drafts, model choices, and project folders for each agent.
- **Bring the whole picture.** Share files, images, and screenshots alongside your message.
- **Pick up where you left off.** Find and continue your Codex conversations.
- **Small when you need it. Spacious when you want it.** Move between a compact panel and a full conversation window.
- **Designed to feel at home on your Mac.** Translucent glass, quiet controls, and a familiar place in your menu bar.

## Meet your new sidekick

1. Download Piko and drag it into **Applications**.
2. Open Piko and click the yellow gecko in your menu bar.
3. Choose your agent, connect your account, and select a project folder.
4. Start a conversation.

Keep Piko in your Dock for quick access, or open it from the yellow gecko in your menu bar.

Piko works with your existing agent installations and accounts. Agent subscriptions are managed by their respective providers.

## Made for Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon

**⌘1–3** switches agents. **⌘ Return** sends your message. **⌘ .** stops a reply.
